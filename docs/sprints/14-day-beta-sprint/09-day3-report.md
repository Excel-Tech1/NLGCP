**Key empirical finding (GLONASS FCN limitation):** Standalone RTCM `1084`
does not carry GLONASS frequency-channel numbers (`DF419`), so both `convbin`
and native `input_rtcm3()` leave carrier-phase cycles at `0.0` (`satwavelen()`

test-only FCNs (`R01: +1`, `R02: -4`) are supplied in `rtcm.nav.geph`, RTKLIB
decodes all four 1084 fine phase-range (`DF401`) fields to within `0.268 mm`
(inside the `±0.279 mm` half-LSB limit).

## 7. Discrepancies, Unsupported Fields, and Limitations

1. **No second non-RTKLIB decoder installed:** RTKLIB v2.4.2-p13 is independent
   C code relative to Python `nlgcp_rtcm_generation`, but shares the Day 2
   layout reference lineage. Validation against a non-RTKLIB decoder (`pyrtcm`,
   BKG BNC, or hardware receiver) remains **NOT PERFORMED**.
2. **RTCM 1005 skipped flags:** RTKLIB `decode_type1005` skips `gps`,
   `glonass`, `galileo`, `reference_station`, `oscillator`, and `quarter_cycle`
   indicators; their bit alignment is verified, not their semantic decoding.
3. **MSM4 header indicators not exposed in RINEX/obs:** `clock_steering`,
   `external_clock`, `smoothing`, `smoothing_interval`, and `reserved` are
   parsed by `decode_msm_head` into `msm_h_t` without downstream semantic
   verification in `convbin` or `save_msm_obs`.
4. **GLONASS 1084 carrier-phase wavelength dependency:** Standalone 1084 cannot
   supply GLONASS FCN; receivers/decoders require accompanying GLONASS
   ephemeris (`1020`) or MSM5/MSM7 extended info (`1085`/`1087`) to convert
   carrier phase ranges to cycles.
5. **RTKLIB `convbin -v 3.02` requires `-scan` for non-default signals:**
   Without `-scan`, `convbin 2.4.2` writes a fixed GPS RINEX 3.02 header
   omitting `2P`; `-scan` resolves all observed MSM signals cleanly.
6. **Lock-time bin verification in RTKLIB:** RTKLIB `save_msm_obs` tracks
   change-in-lock (`lossoflock()`) across epochs rather than exposing the raw
   4-bit `DF402` index on a single epoch; Day 2 unit tests cover all 16 bins
   directly, while multi-epoch lock continuity requires multi-epoch streams.
7. **Authentic RTCM semantic validation remains BLOCKED:** Zero authentic RTCM
   captures exist under `${NLGCP_DATA_ROOT}`.
